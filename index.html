About

Sound as a craft

Vojtěch Komárek is a composer, producer and sound designer. He writes music that serves the story — whether scoring picture, designing the sonic world of a brand, or producing original tracks from the studio up.

His work spans orchestral, electronic and hybrid territory, always shaped around the feeling a project needs.
